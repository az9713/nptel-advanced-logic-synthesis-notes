7. Optimize the datapath and its switching activity

The execution slice rarely writes a result when idle. Even with the same arithmetic and state transition, two optimizations target different costs. Arithmetic restructuring reduces the depth of the active computation. Clock gating reduces clock activity when the slice has no work. Either transformation can change the meaning of the design if control and timing assumptions drift.

Lab 05 · Interpret power reports and inspect clock gating L20 11:21 · L20 45:40

A library can describe leakage conditioned on cell state and internal energy associated with a transition on a related pin. A load transition also charges external capacitance. The power report combines these data with switching rates and state probabilities. Compare reports made with the same workload, annotation scope, clock rate, and averaging interval. If many nets have default activity, an apparent saving may reflect different propagation or coverage rather than a measured workload.

With reset inactive, the slice retains its result when write enable is low. A data-path implementation feeds \(q\) back through a selection network under that condition. Clock gating suppresses unnecessary pulses on a branch of the clock tree. For this slice the gate must pass an edge when reset or write enable is asserted. Gating on write enable alone would suppress a synchronous reset when enable is low. A common positive-edge implementation holds the combined gate request in a latch transparent during the low phase and ANDs the held value with the clock. Because the latch is opaque during the high phase, a request arriving then cannot create a new output edge under the cell's timing contract. Use an integrated clock-gating cell with the proper enable checks and test override. Check the reset edge, the first enabled edge after idle, and the scan mode. A plain combinational AND on a live clock can generate a narrow pulse or truncate the high phase. L20 12:13 · L20 20:24

Static probability measures the fraction of time a signal resides at a logic level. Toggle rate measures transitions per unit time under the selected reporting convention. A node held low for one half of a workload and high for the other half has static probability one half and only one transition. A clock with 50% duty cycle has the same static probability and two transitions every cycle. Use state residence for state-conditioned leakage and event counts for transition-related energy. Optimization scope and resource selection L21 16:33 · L21 43:05

Critical-path synthesis targets a timing priority with rewriting, cell sizing, buffering, or different architecture. Sharing an arithmetic resource can improve area and add a selection stage, while duplicating it can reduce fanout or expose alternate paths. For the decoder, \(x+(s?y:z)\) shares the adder and puts a data select before it. The alternative \(s?(x+y):(x+z)\) duplicates addition and moves selection after it. Both commute with the same fixed-width truncation on each branch. If \(s\) arrives late and \(x,y,z\) arrive early, precomputing both sums can reduce the sensitizable select-to-output path. If the operands arrive late or the selector fans out widely, analyze all arc classes before accepting the additional hardware and switching.

Flattening may expose a shared operator or permit gates to move across a design boundary. It can also change hierarchy and names used by scripts, debug, and proof. Preserve the functional equation as the specification and compare both implementations at the same location, clock, and constraint scope. When two results are observed simultaneously, one shared resource only preserves the contract if it can deliver both results as required by the schedule.

Critical-path synthesis may instead target a single operating bottleneck. First verify that the path is constrained and sensitizable under the intended use. Then identify whether its dominant cost is logic depth, interconnect, or the electrical load of one stage. For example, an output net driving unrelated consumers can justify buffering a separate noncritical branch. This reduces the load seen directly by the critical driver but adds another delay on the buffered branch. Recheck both paths and any shared predecessors after remapping. L21 22:14 · L21 32:47

Extract arithmetic, then balance pipeline stages L22 00:03 · L22 24:38

An arithmetic cone is the part of the combinational network feeding a chosen result through arithmetic operators. A tool may extract a compatible expression tree and choose a datapath implementation across operators. Exposing related operations in the same optimization scope, making widths and signedness explicit, and avoiding unnecessary truncation at internal boundaries can enable a larger match. An internal truncation that is part of the intended computation must survive any rewrite. The modular identities for zero-extended unsigned values do not justify arbitrary rearrangement across a sign change or a saturation operator.

For three one-bit operands \(a_i,b_i,c_i\) at bit position \(i\), a full adder computes parity \(s_i=a_i\oplus b_i\oplus c_i\) and majority \(m_i=a_i b_i+a_i c_i+b_i c_i\), with \(+\) on the right interpreted as Boolean OR. As integers, \(a_i+b_i+c_i=s_i+2m_i\). The identity follows by enumerating the number of ones from zero to three. Weighting each column by \(2^i\) combines the three words into a sum word \(S\) and a carry word \(M\) without immediately propagating carries across columns. One final carry-propagate addition computes \(S+2M\). For six, five, and three, bitwise parity gives zero and bitwise majority gives seven. Thus \(6+5+3=0+2(7)=14\). The compressor saves a full carry propagation when adding three operands. For a fixed-width wraparound output the final low bits preserve the modular sum. If output includes overflow, provision enough bits before reducing width.

In the displayed six-operand datapath, the conventional binary tree resolves five add/subtract operations with carry propagation. A carry-save network can reduce the operand count from six to two by applying four three-to-two compressions, then perform one final carry-propagate addition. Each compression preserves the weighted sum. A subtraction for a fixed-width two's-complement result can use the complemented subtrahend with a correction term of one. Include that correction and the required sign extension when forming compressor columns. L21 48:44

A constant unsigned multiply can sometimes use shifts and addition or subtraction. For example \(15x=(x\ll4)-x\), where \(\ll4\) shifts left by four bits. The slide's parallel-multiplier example starts with a common value \(x\) and computes \(105x\) and \(621x\). The binary expansion gives \(105=64+32+8+1\). The second product reuses that intermediate because \(621=105+512+4\). Thus the remaining terms are shifts by nine and two bits. The products share logic, and the second may inherit the first product's delay. Preserve the intermediate widths and compare area and timing together. L22 13:50

Pipeline registers divide combinational work among cycles. If three consecutive operations take 4, 5, and 3 ns, there are two useful places to put one internal pipeline boundary. The two stage maxima are \(\max(4,8)=8\) ns and \(\max(9,3)=9\) ns, before register costs. Adding a cycle changes latency at the interface. Retiming instead considers moving existing registers while preserving the required input/output behavior and the clock model. Shifting a register across a split can duplicate storage, and resets, enables, and clock domains can forbid moves. Check the sequential contract in Chapter 11. L22 35:18

Account for switching, internal power, and leakage L23 20:07 · L23 48:39

A node with capacitance \(C\) charged from zero to \(V_{DD}\) draws charge \(CV_{DD}\) from the supply. With constant supply voltage the delivered energy is \(CV_{DD}^2\). Half is stored and half dissipates during the charging transition in a resistive model. The stored half dissipates on discharge. Define \(\alpha\) as the expected number of full-swing charging events per clock cycle, and \(f\) as the clock frequency in hertz. Then average switching power in watts is \(P_{sw}=\alpha C V_{DD}^2 f\). With \(C=40\) fF, \(V_{DD}=0.8\) V, \(f=1\) GHz, and \(\alpha=0.25\), the estimate is \(6.4\,\mu\mathrm W\). If activity instead counts both transition directions, the steady-state charge-event rate is half the total transition rate under balanced activity.

The slide derives this from the supply-current integral. Over observation time \(T_{obs}\), let \(i_{DD}(t)\) be the supply current attributable to charging the external load and \(n_{\uparrow}\) the number of full-swing charging events. The integral \(\int_0^{T_{obs}}i_{DD}(t)\,dt\) is \(n_{\uparrow}CV_{DD}\). Multiplying by \(V_{DD}/T_{obs}\) gives power, with \(n_{\uparrow}/T_{obs}=\alpha f\). If measured supply current also includes internal and leakage components, the same integral measures their energy as well. L23 16:55

Internal power includes energy dissipation within a cell, such as charging internal nodes and transient conduction through pull-up and pull-down. Leakage power depends on the cell state and corner even with no clock transitions. A VCD stores event changes over time. A SAIF file typically summarizes activity and state residence over an interval. A trace-derived estimate can miss glitches when simulation lacks delays, miss modes when the workload lacks them, and miss hierarchy when annotation names do not match. Record both the exercised workload and the fraction of intended nets with annotation. Preserve the denominator when calculating the effect of clock gating. Synopsys Liberty Reference Manual

For an original leakage example, suppose a cell dissipates 2 nW in a state occupied 75% of the time and 10 nW otherwise at the same corner. The average leakage is \(0.75(2)+0.25(10)=4\) nW. For transition energy, multiply the energy per event by the rate of that event. If the report counts both edges of a full-swing node and the observation interval includes many balanced transitions, external-capacitance power is approximately half the total edge rate times \(CV_{DD}^2\). A clock that sees one full cycle has two edges but only one charge event from the supply. L23 29:26 · L23 42:28

The activity-generation table distinguishes what can be observed at each simulation level. RTL activity describes signals that survive the abstraction boundary and needs a correspondence when annotated onto a mapped design. A mapped zero-delay simulation exposes internal nodes but misses pulses caused by unequal physical path delays. A timing-annotated mapped simulation can capture such pulses under its delay model and stimulus. Laboratory and solved problems

python code/lab.py datapath checks every input combination for three four-bit operands, including the most significant carry. It also checks the example dynamic-power calculation.

  1. How much ideal dynamic power can gating save? If a \(40\) fF clock branch charges once per cycle at \(0.8\) V and \(1\) GHz, its ungated switching power is \(25.6\,\mu\mathrm W\). If the gate passes exactly one pulse in four, the branch's ideal switching power is \(6.4\,\mu\mathrm W\). The \(19.2\,\mu\mathrm W\) difference excludes the gate, enable activity, test mode, and any cells outside the gated branch.
  2. When does reassociation change an answer? In an unsigned saturating arithmetic model with range 0 to 15, consider \((12+9)-8\) when the first sum saturates. It gives \(15-8=7\). Computing \(12+(9-8)\) gives 13. A synthesis transformation that assumes modular or exact addition at that boundary is invalid for the original saturating model.
